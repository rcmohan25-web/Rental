# Honesty rules (show in the app and in the report)

1. Payments are manual (eSewa, Khalti or bank transfer, paid to the Sapati account by scanning a static QR). Every payment screen says "Manual payment (prototype)". No gateway, escrow or insurance.
2. Payouts and refunds are recorded, not executed. An admin pays outside the app and records the reference.
3. Identity checks are manual. "Verified" means checked by an admin. No automated face matching or liveness detection is claimed.
4. Legal action is an offline process. The app records evidence, deadlines and case status only.
5. Terms and Conditions are templates, marked "prototype, not legal advice", and are reviewed by Sapati's legal team before real use.
6. Ticking a box is a recorded acceptance, not a certified e-signature.
7. Demo data is fake or consented. Never put real strangers' IDs or faces in screenshots or the report.
8. The identity-misuse clause is a template. Name no specific law or penalty until Sapati's legal team confirms it.
